# Vanquis Banking Group

> Vanquis Banking Group plc (formerly Provident Financial) is a UK specialist non-prime lender and savings bank. Its banking subsidiary, Vanquis Bank Limited, is an FCA-authorised ASPSP (FS Register no. 221156) and a voluntary UK Open Banking participant (not one of the CMA9). It exposes the OBIE Read/Write v3.1.10 API family — Account & Transaction (AIS), Payment Initiation (PIS), Confirmation of Funds (CBPII) — plus Dynamic Client Registration, secured under the PSD2 / FAPI profile (OAuth2/OIDC, mutual-TLS, SCA) and onboarded through the Open Banking Directory. The production API host is provisioned per TPP at onboarding and is not public; the OpenAPIs here are the shared OBIE standard contracts Vanquis conforms to.

## APIs
- [Account and Transaction API (AIS)](https://www.vanquis.com/developer-portal/): OBIE Read/Write AISP v3.1.10 — account, balance, transaction, statement and party data with PSU consent.
- [Payment Initiation API (PIS)](https://www.vanquis.com/developer-portal/): OBIE Read/Write PISP v3.1.10 — domestic/international/file payments, scheduled payments and standing orders.
- [Confirmation of Funds API (CBPII)](https://www.vanquis.com/developer-portal/): OBIE Read/Write CBPII v3.1.10 — confirm availability of funds on a card-based payment instrument.
- [Dynamic Client Registration](https://www.vanquis.com/developer-portal/): OBIE/OpenID DCR v1.0.0-rc2 — programmatic OAuth client registration with OBIE/eIDAS certificates.
